# Introduction

!!! abstract "Verify your computer"

You have read my getting started notes, and you know what sort of computer you need. But how do you verify if your home computer is suitable for this module and programme?

In our courses, desktop virtualization is done using _VMware Workstation_. This is education not training, however, I do audit and maintain some cross compatibility with industry certifications. I am going to assume you have basic competence with Windows.

There are other hypervisors, they will work for all activities.

For some e-delivery students, I am using Azure Labs and this will use Hyper-V only.

On campus, we use the University laboratories, you will use VMWare Workstation only.

When learning, we will use Windows GUI as always. However, all small data centre work is done using scripting; we figure out how to do something and then we write a script to do it. Whenever we do something in the GUI, we will also look at the script for doing the same task. When you are recording your work, you only record the scripts, not the GUI (no or few screenshots!).  

Over the years, there have been many changes in the way students carry out the practical exercises. As with all things in IT, the platforms we work with are characterized by change and instability; with each patch release, something that worked before no longer does. The exercises I demonstrate worked in the way I demonstrate, at a point in time, on the versions of code I show. You may need to adapt and figure out solutions where an example no longer works.

This is normal in our business, get used to it!

After reading those notes, you know what kind of computer you need and we will do all our exercises based on virtualization. There are some specific requirements before you can use your Windows 10/11 computer as a virtualization platform. The first thing we need to do is to understand what hardware is in the computer.
